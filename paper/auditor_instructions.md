# Auditor instructions

Thank you for helping. You will read short pieces of old American newspapers and answer one YES/NO question about each. There are two pages: one for 1872 (260 cards) and one for 1859 (110 cards). You are checking someone else's answers, but you will not see them. Your own honest judgment is what we need.

## The task

The text is machine-read from scans, so it has typos and garbled words. Ignore that.

At the top of each page are the rules, exactly as the original labelers received them. Apply them to every article. Read them carefully once before you start, and again whenever you are unsure. They are the only rules: please do not add your own.

## Time and sessions

Expect about 40 to 60 seconds per article, about 4 to 5 hours in all. Work in sessions of about 20 minutes, with breaks. Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can close the page and come back later on the same device and browser.

## Rules

- Do not search the web for the text or the topic.
- Do not use AI tools, translators or text assistants.
- Judge each article on its own, and do not try to remember earlier ones.
- Garbled OCR is normal. If you honestly cannot tell, pick the answer you think is more likely.
- Do not discuss the articles with anyone until the work is done.
- Do not open this project's repository, its OSF page or any published results until you have sent your final answers.

## Sending your answers

1. Open a page file (audit.html) in your browser. A phone or a laptop both work.
2. Tap YES or NO, and the next article appears. Use Back to change an answer.
3. When you finish, or at the end of any session, tap "Download answers". A file ending in .tsv is saved.
4. Email or message that file to the person who sent you the page. Partial progress is fine, and so is sending it again later with more answers.
